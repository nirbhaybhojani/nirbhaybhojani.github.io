02 PERSONAL STATEMENT
I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar, holding a CGPA of 9.84 and department Rank 1. My passion lies at the intersection of electromagnetic theory and practical system design — specifically in millimeter-wave antenna engineering, metasurface-based polarization control, and RF integrated circuit design.

My technical journey has taken me from designing circularly polarized antennas at ISRO's Space Application Center to collaborating with IIT BHU on reconfigurable metasurfaces, and from designing a 28 GHz ME dipole antenna for satellite communications to leading a student satellite communication subsystem. I approach every project with rigor, seeking not just functional designs but ones that push the boundaries of bandwidth, axial ratio, and integration.

Beyond the lab, I serve as Vice Chair of the IEEE Signal Processing Society Student Branch Chapter at PDEU, where I work to build a culture of research and technical excellence. I have published a patent, submitted journal papers to IEEE TAP, and am actively expanding my work into RFIC design. My goal is to pursue a graduate degree (MS) specializing in RFIC or RF System Design at a research-focused institution, where I can contribute meaningfully to the future of wireless and satellite communication systems.
GOAL: MS in RFIC / RF System Design — targeting research-focused graduate programs

Reconfigurable reflective metasurface designed in collaboration with IIT BHU and PDEU. Uses a varactor diode to switch between polarization conversion rotation (PCR) mode and LP to CP conversion mode. Designed on FR4 substrate with 10 mm periodicity.
