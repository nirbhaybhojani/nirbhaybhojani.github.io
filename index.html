01 ABOUT
I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Ahmedabad, holding a CGPA of 9.84 and standing Rank 1 in the ECE department. My research spans millimeter-wave antenna design, metasurfaces for polarization conversion, and RF circuit design using industry-standard tools including Ansys HFSS, CST, and ADS.

I interned at the Space Application Center (SAC), ISRO, where I designed circularly polarized antennas and built an antenna tracking system. I am currently working on multiple journal and conference papers, and collaborating with IIT BHU on metasurface research. I serve as Vice Chair of the IEEE SPS Student Branch Chapter at PDEU and am pursuing graduate studies in RFIC or RF System Design.

Reconfigurable reflective metasurface designed in collaboration with IIT BHU and PDEU. Uses a varactor diode to switch between polarization conversion rotation (PCR) mode and LP to CP conversion mode. Designed on FR4 substrate with 10 mm periodicity.
